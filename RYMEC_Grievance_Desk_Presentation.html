RYMEC Institutional Grievance Redressal System
1. Introduction & Institutional Background
Institutional Context & Mandate
  • RYMEC Ballari: Established in 1980 under V.V. Sangha, catering to over 3,500+ undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students across 8 distinct academic departments.
  • Regulatory Mandate: AICTE and UGC regulations mandate a transparent, non-discriminatory grievance redressal mechanism with defined escalation timelines for higher educational institutions.
  • Current Legacy Workflow: Grievances were historically handled via physical drop-boxes, handwritten complaints, or fragmented email threads, resulting in severe visibility gaps and lost accountability.
Identified Bottlenecks & Motivation
  • Fear of Academic Reprisal: Students hesitate to report sensitive infrastructure, ragging, harassment, or evaluation issues due to personal identity exposure.
  • Unmonitored Delays & SLA Breaches: No automated tracking exists to notify heads of departments (HODs) or Principal when urgent grievances remain unaddressed.
  • Duplicate & Clustered Reports: Multiple students submit individual tickets for common campus faults (e.g., Wi-Fi, lab equipment), overwhelming the support desk.
  • Absence of Audit Trail: Lack of verifiable, tamper-evident logs to monitor administrative actions, leading to student mistrust.

Mathematical Foundation
5. Mathematical Formulations & Algorithmic Logic
1. Parametric Circular Orbit Transformation (Component 89 OTP)
x_i = r · cos(θ_i) - x_{orig, i},   y_i = r · sin(θ_i),   where θ_i = -π/2 + i · (2π/6),   r = 52px

Calculates exact translation offsets from horizontal row positions x_{orig} ∈ {-120, -72, -24, +24, +72, +120}px. Ensures seamless 60 FPS transitions without DOM re-renders.

2. Duplicate Grievance Detection (Jaccard Token Similarity)
J(A, B) = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B| = |A ∩ B| / (|A| + |B| - |A ∩ B|),   Trigger Alert if J(A, B) ≥ 0.65

Tokenizes and normalizes complaint titles and descriptions after removing stop-words. Clusters overlapping grievances under a single master issue to streamline departmental resolution.

3. Dynamic SLA Risk & Escalation Scoring
RiskScore(t) = ((t - t_created) / SLA_target) · W_severity,   W_High = 1.5,   W_Med = 1.0,   W_Low = 0.5

When RiskScore(t) ≥ 1.0, the grievance is automatically escalated to the Principal's review deck with automated email alerts dispatched via institutional SMTP.
